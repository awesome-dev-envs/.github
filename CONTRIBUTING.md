# Contributing

Thank you for helping Awesome Development Environments. By participating, you agree to our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

This organization maintains a base list for installing **common tools for the local development environment**.
The applications will be downloaded from official stores; that is, we do not provide or maintain the applications themselves.

## How to Contribute

1. Choose the stack that best matches your expertise and interests.
2. Open a discussion to align your ideas with the maintainers and community.
3. Create an issue once the scope and requirements are clear.
4. Create a feature or bugfix branch based on the issue.
5. Implement and test your changes.
6. Open a Pull Request for review and feedback.

### Guidelines

- Keep changes focused and self-explained.
- Follow the existing project structure and conventions.
- Be respectful and collaborative during discussions and reviews.

## Project Maintenance Policy

To keep the project healthy and active, maintainers reserve the right to close, archive, transfer, or remove discussions and issues when necessary.  
Discussions and issues with no response or activity from their owner for more than 3 months may be closed, reassigned to another contributor, or removed at the maintainers' discretion.
